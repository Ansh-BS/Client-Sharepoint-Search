A — Quiet Utility

The design you have now, tightened. Result cards become ruled rows on a single surface, so ten matches read as one list instead of ten objects. Admin and Log out drop into a thin utility bar and stop competing with the search box. Indigo still means selection, teal still means the one action worth taking. Lowest-risk of the three: nothing here is unfamiliar to staff who use it today.
